## ADDING A COMMENT TO A file.html:

After writing your comment, move the cursor on the line where your commnet starts and press on your kyeboard COMMAND+/, and press enter. Your command will transform your written text into a proper .html comment.

### COLSPAN: Cell attributes <th> and <td>. It will help us to merge 2 or more cells in a row.

                          SYNTAX:

<table>
<tr>
<th colspan="2"><h1>MY SHOPPING LIST</h1></th></tr>
<tr>
<th><h4>ELECTRONICS</h4></th>
<th><h5>CONSOLES</h5></th>
<th><h6>FOOD</h6></th></tr>

### ROWSPAN: cell attributes <th> and <td>. It will merge 2 or more cells in columns.

### To add color to my headlines within the <th> tag, type:

<th style="color:green;">MANGO</th> (green or any other color you need).
If I have an attribute already typed within my <th rowspan="4"> for example. I just need to leave a space and then to add my next attribute, color for example: <th rowspan="4" style="color:red;">MANGO</th><br> 
The semicolon (;) will always be used at the end of a style attribute only.

### ONLY ON files.html:

If I need to add 3 or more <th>-</th> tags, I can simple type th\*4 (press TAB, or ENTER) and there I will have the numbers of <th> tags I need.

## REMEMBER when I have to add data within my chart to type:

<td>Information Required</td>; <th>...</th> is just for headers.

# Adding a class:

td.data+TAB, becomes <td class="data"></td>

# Nesting:

tr>td\*3 + TAB, creates a whole row with three cells inside at once.

# The Boilerplate:

If I start a brand new file and just type ! (and press TAB), it will create the entire HTML structure (<html>, <head>, <body>) for me instantly.
